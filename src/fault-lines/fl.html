        Fault Lines

        An 11 kV feeder has tripped and locked out, and everyone on it is off. The fault is in one section. Find it with as few switching operations as you can, then isolate it and get everyone else back on.

        1. Read the clues. Protection flags, fault passage indicators (FPIs flag when fault current passed through them) and customer calls.
        2. Test. Open a switch, then close the breaker. If it holds, the fault is beyond the open switch. If it trips, the fault is before it, and it costs points.
        3. Isolate and restore. Open the switches either side of the faulted section. Close the breaker for the sections before it, and the normally open point for the sections beyond it.
        4. Declare. Tap the faulted section and declare the fault there.

        Today's puzzle is the same for everyone. You get one go, and your result grid shares how you did without giving the answer away.         Spurs branch off the main line through their own spur switch, and the fault can be on a spur too: open its spur switch to test or isolate it. Square switches are remote: 1 step. AR switches are auto-reclosers: they trip before your breaker does. Round ones are manual: the crew drives out from the depot, 2 steps and 15 minutes. Par is what an expert would need.
